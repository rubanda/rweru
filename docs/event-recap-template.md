# Event recap template

Publish within 48 hours of every community event, as a new post in `lib/blog-data.ts`
(category: `"Community"`). Keep it short — this is a recap, not an essay.

```md
Title: Recap: {Event title} — {Month} edition

## What happened
One or two sentences: who showed up, what the format was, how many people attended
(a real headcount, not an estimate).

## {N} takeaways
1. {Concrete thing someone learned or shipped}
2. {Concrete thing someone learned or shipped}
3. {Concrete thing someone learned or shipped}

## Shoutouts
Name the volunteers who hosted, mentored, or helped run logistics. This is what turns
attendees into volunteers — public recognition, every time.

## What's next
Link directly to the next upcoming event on /community, plus the RSVP link.
```

Checklist before publishing:

- [ ] At least 2 photos from the event (with consent)
- [ ] Named shoutouts for every volunteer who helped
- [ ] A direct link to the next event's RSVP
- [ ] Cross-posted as 1 short update to the community chat channel

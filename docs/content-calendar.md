# RweruSynapse — 90-day editorial calendar

Companion to the growth plan. Five content pillars, rotating weekly. Update the `status`
column as posts move through the pipeline. Add real posts to `lib/blog-data.ts` (or the
CMS once one is connected — see "CMS decision" in the growth plan) as they're written.

Pillars:

- **Build log** — how we built X for a real client (Masata, Eza Hotel, NJC, jFountain)
- **Research note** — African digital infrastructure, payments, connectivity constraints
- **Community recap** — writeup after every workshop / ship night, published within 48h
- **Operator playbook** — practical guides for founders/operators in Rwanda
- **Explainer (AI-search bait)** — direct-answer page structured for AI Overviews / chat answer engines
